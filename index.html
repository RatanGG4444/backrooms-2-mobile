How to Play

MOUSE Look Around
SHIFT Sprint
CTRL Crouch

ESC Menu

Survival Tips:

  • Conserve your flashlight battery - darkness is deadly
  • Monitor your sanity - low sanity causes hallucinations
  • Don't run unless necessary - entities are attracted to noise
  • Stick together with your team for safety
  • Avoid direct eye contact with entities
  • Search for almond water to restore sanity
