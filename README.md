## 🌟 Features

- 🎮 **4-Player Co-op** - Team up with friends to survive
- 🎤 **Proximity Voice Chat** - Communicate with nearby players
- 🗺️ **6 Unique Levels** - Procedurally generated environments
- 👻 **7 Deadly Entities** - Each with unique AI behaviors
- 🧠 **Sanity System** - Manage your mental state to survive
- 💡 **Dynamic Lighting** - Use flashlights strategically
- 📦 **Item System** - Find almond water, batteries, and more
- 🎨 **Custom Level Editor** - Create and share your own levels
- 👁️ **Spectator Mode** - Watch your friends after death

## 🎯 How to Play

### Controls
- **W A S D** - Move
- **Mouse** - Look around
- **Shift** - Sprint (drains stamina)
- **Ctrl** - Crouch
- **F** - Toggle flashlight
- **E** - Use item
- **V** - Push to talk
- **Tab** - Inventory
- **ESC** - Menu

### Survival Tips
1. 💡 Conserve flashlight battery
2. 🧠 Monitor your sanity level
3. 🔇 Avoid making noise
4. 👥 Stay close to teammates
5. 👁️ Don't look at entities
6. 🥤 Find almond water to restore sanity
